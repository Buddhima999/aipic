Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

About AIpiC

AIpiC is operated by the site owner, who can be contacted at dishanthabuddhima@gmail.com. This policy describes information handled when you browse or download our AI-generated pictures and wallpapers.

Browsing and downloads

Visitors do not need an account or need to provide an email address to download images. We store a total download count for each image. This counter does not store a list of individual downloaders.

Technical information

Requests to the website involve technical information, such as your IP address, requested address, and browser information. Our hosting provider may process this information in operational and security logs. The backend also uses IP-based rate limiting to help prevent excessive requests.

Admin accounts

Admin email addresses and hashed passwords are stored for authentication. Admin login tokens are held temporarily in browser memory and expire after two hours. The current app does not store these tokens in cookies or local storage.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments you send. We use this information to respond to your request and handle image-related concerns. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

Service providers

  • Vercel hosts the website and backend.
  • MongoDB Atlas stores image files, image details, and admin account information.
  • Google Fonts supplies website fonts. Your browser contacts Google when loading these fonts.
  • Gmail handles messages sent to our contact address.

These providers may process information on servers outside your country, according to their services and applicable policies.

Advertising and analytics

Google AdSense and visitor analytics are currently disabled on AIpiC. We will update this policy before enabling advertising or additional tracking.

Retention

Image records remain until removed by the administrator. Admin account records remain until deleted. Contact messages are retained as needed to respond and resolve the request. Hosting log retention depends on the provider and the configured service plan.

Privacy requests

To ask about information relating to you, or request access, correction, or deletion where applicable, contact dishanthabuddhima@gmail.com . We may need additional information to identify the relevant records and verify your request.

Policy changes

We may update this page when our services or data practices change. The date above indicates the latest update.
