Privacy policy

AIpiC stores image metadata and admin account details in MongoDB. Admin passwords are hashed. The download counter records totals, not visitor accounts. Hosting providers may process request information such as IP addresses and browser details in operational logs.

Before enabling advertising, replace this draft with an accurate policy identifying the operator, contact details, actual providers, retention periods, user rights, and applicable consent choices. Google advertising may use cookies or similar technologies for ad delivery, measurement, and personalization. See Google’s partner-site privacy information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. This starter does not implement a consent management platform.
